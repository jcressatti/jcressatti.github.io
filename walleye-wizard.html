Walleye Wizard

Real-Time Tournament Management & Catch Logging App

Next.js Supabase PWA / Web App

The Problem

Managing multi-angler fishing tournaments during annual trips usually means dealing with messy paper logs, fragmented text threads, and delayed end-of-day scoring updates.

The Solution

Walleye Wizard provides a mobile-friendly web platform where anglers can record catches instantly in the field. The platform automatically tallies scores, computes tournament standings, and powers a real-time leaderboard.

Key Features

  • Instant Catch Logging: Quick entry for species, measurement, angler, and timestamp.
  • Dynamic Leaderboard: Live score updates and standings calculation.
  • Field-Ready Design: Optimized for quick mobile entry on the water.
